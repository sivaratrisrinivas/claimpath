    The two sample cases are synthetic. The rules are real (each links to official text). The "Similar real past cases" panel is separate context: it never changes the verdict.

    India — Insurance Ombudsman award summaries, 2009–2012. Council for Insurance Ombudsmen compilations published on cioins.co.in (Mediclaim-Book12/13/14/16/17 PDFs). I found no licence statement on the site; they are public official documents, excerpted and abbreviated here with complainant names removed. The CIO no longer publishes new awards publicly (parties download them with an OTP), so nothing newer is available. These awards pre-date the Ombudsman Rules 2017 and the IRDAI 2024 Master Circular (e.g. today's 60-month moratorium and 36-month PED cap), so they show how disputes were argued, not how they would be decided today.

    USA — California DMHC Independent Medical Review determinations, 2024–2026. CHHS Open Data Portal (search tool: wpso.dmhc.ca.gov/imr). One portal page lists Creative Commons Attribution, the other "CHHS Terms of Use"; both permit public use with attribution. These are California state-regulated plans, not ERISA self-funded plans, and contain no policy clause, dates or dollar amounts.

    How the sample was made. Cases were picked by keyword and balanced by outcome, and linked to the nearest sample clause automatically, so counts are NOT a success rate and some links are approximate. The India text was OCR'd from PDFs and may contain errors. Not legal or medical advice.
